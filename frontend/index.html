Deleting a round permanently removes its matches and every price snapshot, signal and score for them. It disappears at once and the data is cleared in the background. A round that has never been downloaded asks for an extra confirmation before it can be deleted. "Downloaded" means the CSV was served by the app, not that your browser saved it. The size per round is an estimate. Space freed by a delete is reused by new data right away, but the database size meter only drops after "Reclaim disk space".

Margin distribution over time

Pinnacle's margin (overround) and how it is spread across each pick. A pick's margin is its price-implied probability minus its de-vigged fair probability, in percentage points; the picks add up to the total margin. The second chart shows each pick's share of that total. Descriptive only: the split is computed from the prices with the same power de-vig as the fair probabilities, so it is fixed by the prices rather than a separate signal, and a backtests on up to 175 matches found no predictive value in it (RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md section 14). For the AH and total markets the line itself can move, which changes what is being priced.

Total goals: line + Over/Under odds

Purple (left axis) is the goal-total line itself (e.g. 2.5 → 3.0) — a structural shift, not just a price move. Green/orange (right axis) are the Over/Under decimal odds. Line + Over moving together = sharp money expects more goals; line steady with odds drifting alone is more likely routine.

Total goals: Over/Under fair probability (%)

Same de-vig treatment as the 1X2 and AH probability charts, applied to the Over/Under prices at whatever total line is currently quoted — vig removed, so this is the market's real view of how likely Over/Under is, not the raw price. Reads cleanly only while the line itself hasn't moved (same caveat as the AH chart - a shifted line changes what's being priced).
